# clouds

A glide through broken cumulus at golden hour, nothing captured: clouds are billowy density fields, each splat generated on the GPU from its index and the time and lit by marching toward the sun. Drawn by [spark-radix][radix] on three.js's `WebGPURenderer`.

Drag to orbit, right-drag or shift-drag to pan, scroll to move; let go and the glide eases back in.

[radix]: https://github.com/asundqui/radix-test
