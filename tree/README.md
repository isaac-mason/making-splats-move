# tree

A tree, nothing captured: grown from a seed on the CPU, its canopy sprays of soft-edged splat leaves, blown by the wind. Drawn by [spark-radix][radix] on three.js's `WebGPURenderer`.

Drag to orbit, right-drag or shift-drag to pan, scroll to move in.

[radix]: https://github.com/asundqui/radix-test
