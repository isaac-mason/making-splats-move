# snowglobe

The anime city's street crossing as a snow globe: a diorama under glass with GPU-simulated snow that settles on roofs and streets. Drawn by [Radix][radix] on three.js's `WebGPURenderer`.

Space shakes it (hold to keep shaking); drag to orbit, right-drag or shift-drag to pan, scroll to move in. Without compute (WebGL2) the snow just falls.

[radix]: https://sparkjs.dev/radix/
