# fireworks

A fireworks show over dark hills under a starry night, nothing captured or simulated: eight kinds of shell climb, burst and fall, every spark a splat worked out from the time. Drawn by [Radix][radix] on three.js's `WebGPURenderer`.

Drag to orbit, right-drag or shift-drag to pan, scroll to move in.

[radix]: https://sparkjs.dev/radix/
