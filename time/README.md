# time

The [`avatars/`](../avatars) school courtyard relit through a whole day: a sun that crosses and sets, shadows across the lawn, the blue hour and moonlight. Normals, sky and shadows are worked out from the splats on load. Drawn by [Radix][radix].

The day plays on its own; the bar at the bottom scrubs it, space pauses, and dragging looks around.

[radix]: https://sparkjs.dev/radix/
