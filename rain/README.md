# rain

A steady downpour over the [`avatars/`](../avatars) school courtyard: streaking drops and splashes, soaked shiny paving and roofs, puddles with rings, dry under the trees. Where rain reaches is worked out on load from the splats and a collision mesh. Drawn by [Radix][radix]; drag to look around.

[radix]: https://sparkjs.dev/radix/
